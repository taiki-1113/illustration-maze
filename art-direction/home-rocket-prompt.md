## プロンプト

Extract and faithfully recreate ONLY the small cute flying rocket illustration in the home page's upper-right area of this reference image, as one standalone website decoration with a genuinely transparent background. Cream body, vermilion red fins and nose, round blue window, pointing diagonally upper-right, soft cream exhaust sweeping left into one compact dark petrol/teal scalloped cloud with a few small ochre stars. Retro mid-century children's printed book, subtle gouache/print grain, elegant cream teal red ochre palette. Match the reference rocket closely. Tightly compose with about 5% transparent padding. No words, lettering, numbers, UI, frame, cards, maze, website screenshot, page background, paper rectangle, drop shadow or any other objects. Transparent background around the entire illustration; the teal cloud is part of the illustration.
