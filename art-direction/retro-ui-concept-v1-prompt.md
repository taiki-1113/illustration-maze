No laptops, phones, hands, room scene, skew, perspective or fake browser chrome. Clearly show both full page layouts plus a narrow integrated bottom strip showing button details in the same style.
Art direction: a sophisticated mid-century Japanese printed children's activity book and a tasteful independent stationery shop. Warm ivory paper background with extremely subtle paper grain, strong deep ink-brown typography, muted petrol/teal green as the primary accent, warm vermilion for a small secondary accent, ochre for achievement stamps. Rich, deliberate restrained palette, ample cream negative space. Charming editorial composition, clean modern usability. Print-like thin double-rule borders and a few registration-style ornaments, small stamped number badges, gentle hard 2px offset button shadows, tactile printed-paper cards. Corners modestly rounded, NOT giant pill-shaped UI. Beautiful heavy Japanese rounded display lettering for titles, clear readable Japanese sans for all controls, tasteful tiny Roman caps only for subtle section labels. Not sepia/dirty/distressed, not low contrast washed-out beige, not cheap clipart, not generic dashboard UI, not ornate vintage restaurant menu, no frills clutter.
